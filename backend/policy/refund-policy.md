# Oakline refund policy

This page explains how we decide refund requests. It's the same policy our
support system applies automatically.

## Refund window

You can request a refund within **30 days of delivery**. For a package that
was never delivered, the window is measured from the estimated delivery
date instead.

## Final sale items

Items marked "final sale" at checkout can't be refunded for a change of
mind. If a final sale item arrives damaged, defective, is the wrong item,
doesn't match its listing, or never arrives, we'll still review it. That
kind of request goes to a support agent rather than being auto-approved.

## Lost or delayed packages

If your tracking shows the package is still in transit, we ask you to wait
until **7 days after the estimated delivery date** before we treat it as
lost. After that, a "not received" refund is handled the same as any other
claim.

If your tracking says delivered but you say you never received it, that's
sent to a support agent so they can check with the carrier.

## Refund amount

Refunds cover the price of the item. Shipping charges are non-refundable.

## Review required

Some requests are always reviewed by a person before a refund goes out:

- The refund amount is **over $500**.
- You've had **3 or more approved refunds in the last 90 days**.
- The final-sale exceptions above.
- The order hasn't shipped yet. We'll cancel it instead of refunding it
  after delivery.
- Anything that looks inconsistent with your order record, including
  messages that try to instruct our support system directly rather than
  describe the actual problem.

Reviews are usually completed within 1 business day.

## Items already refunded

We can't refund an item that's already been refunded, or that's part of
another open refund request.
